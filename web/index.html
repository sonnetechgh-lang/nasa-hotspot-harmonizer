      Two sensors, one record

      Raw MODIS and VIIRS disagree several-fold. The harmonized record joins them without a jump when VIIRS starts in 2012.

      Units: active cell-days, a 0.025° grid cell with fire on a day. How the harmonization works

      About the data

      Active cell-days
      The unit of burning: a 0.025° grid cell (about 2.8 km) with at least one fire on a day.
      Harmonized
      MODIS times a monthly factor before VIIRS starts (2012), VIIRS after. One record, in VIIRS-equivalent units.
      Normal
      The average for each month over the reference years. The usual range is the middle 80% of them.

      How the outlook works

      Forecast
      Each month is half the average of the same month over the last 5 years, half the 15-season trend line.
      Likely range
      Where 8 in 10 past forecasts made at this time of year landed.
      Limits
      Past fires only. Rainfall, policy and land-use changes are not included.
